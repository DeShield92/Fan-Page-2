The CIA Triad


The CIA Triad is a foundational model for information security that stands for Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability.



  • Confidentiality: Prevents unauthorized disclosure of sensitive or private information and ensures data is accessible only to those who are authorized.

  • Integrity: Guarantees that data is accurate, trustworthy, and has not been altered or tampered with in an unauthorized way during storage or transmission.

  • Availability: Ensures that systems, networks, and data are up, running, and accessible to authorized users when needed.
